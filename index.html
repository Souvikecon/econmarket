Scope and sources

Small, current, and verifiable.

The directory monitors the top 10 US economics departments and the top 8 UK university economics departments in the latest configured IDEAS/RePEc rankings. Research institutes and business schools are excluded.

A candidate is included only when an official department page identifies a macroeconomic field and a working job market paper link can be found. Macroeconomics includes international macro, monetary economics, macro-finance, growth, and closely related aggregate topics.
